Chapter 01

First Principles & Domain Foundations

ISO 19152 Ontology

Under India's Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP), textual Record of Rights (RoR) digitization is ~95% complete. However, spatial cadastral maps and Field Measurement Books (F-Lines / FMBs) remain fragmented. Merely converting colonial paper maps to digital vectors creates the Digitization Paradox: freezing century-old survey inaccuracies with high-precision digital certainty.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ | THE DIGITIZATION PARADOX | | | | 1920 Paper Cadastre 2026 Direct Vectorization Physical Ground Occupation | | [ Shrinkage & Folds ] --> [ High-Precision GeoJSON ] Clogs 66% of Indian Civil Litigation | +-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Presumptive vs. Conclusive Title

Under India's Registration Act of 1908, deed registration registers the transaction, not the title. Plot-Armor enables the shift to conclusive title (Torrens system) by validating physical ground-truth against institutional records before legal state transition.

ISO 19152-1 LADM Core Principles

Strictly decouples the physical space (LA_SpatialUnit) from human rights (LA_Party & LA_RRR) and surveying evidence (LA_SpatialSource), preventing corrupt title cascades.

Chapter 04

TEE Principles & Hardware Cryptography

Anti-Spoofing Silicon Gating

To prevent "desk surveys" (fabricating coordinates from home) and GPS software spoofing, Plot-Armor binds surveyor identity directly to mobile device Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) via the WebAuthn / FIDO2 standard.

Silicon Key Generation

Asymmetric private keys ($K_{\text{priv}}$) are generated in Apple Secure Enclave or Android StrongBox. The key never leaves the hardware chip.

Telemetry Attestation

Every payload binds raw NMEA sentences, timestamp, HDOP/PDOP dilution of precision, and carrier-phase satellite telemetry into the signed digest.

Zero On-Chain PII

Complies with DPDP Act 2023 & Section 16. No names, phone numbers, or coordinates enter the ledger—only irreversible SHA-256 state hashes.

Mathematics Behind the Backend Engines

Spatial SDI Formulas
Intersection over Union (Jaccard Index):
$$\text{IoU}(A, B) = \frac{\text{Area}(A \cap B)}{\text{Area}(A \cup B)} = \frac{\text{Area}(A \cap B)}{\text{Area}(A) + \text{Area}(B) - \text{Area}(A \cap B)}$$
Haversine Geodesic Centroid Displacement:
$$a = \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos(\phi_A)\cos(\phi_B)\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right), \quad D_{\text{centroid}} = 2 R \cdot \text{atan2}\left(\sqrt{a}, \sqrt{1-a}\right)$$
Hausdorff Distance Approximation & Risk Score:
$$H_{\text{approx}} = D_{\text{centroid}} \times \left(1 + \frac{|\text{Area}(A) - \text{Area}(B)|}{\text{Area}(A)}\right), \quad \mathbf{Risk\_Score = (1 - \text{IoU}) \times 100}$$

Formal Auditor Interrogation Matrix

Q1: How does Plot-Armor prevent GPS spoofing and desk surveys? ▼
By utilizing the FIDO2/WebAuthn standard on hardware Secure Enclaves / StrongBox chips, raw satellite NMEA sentences and ephemeris dilution metrics are cryptographically signed at the silicon layer. The private key cannot be accessed or spoofed by mock location software.
Q2: Does storing land data on a blockchain violate the DPDP Act 2023? ▼
No. Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII), names, Aadhaar numbers, or raw coordinate arrays are ever placed on-chain. Only irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic digests are anchored to the ledger.
Q3: Why use Intersection over Union (IoU) instead of standard area delta? ▼
Because of the Equal Area Paradox: a 1-acre plot can shift 50 meters into a neighbor's property and retain identical area. IoU measures rotational and translational overlap, detecting true physical encroachment.
Q4: Can a rogue Revenue Officer falsely certify an encroached boundary? ▼
No. Under the Hyperledger consortium endorsement policy AND('RevenueMSP', 'SurveyMSP'), state transitions require multi-party cryptographic sign-off. A rogue officer's modification without survey department peer endorsement is rejected.
